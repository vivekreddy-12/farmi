Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data farmin (“we”, “us”) collects when you use farmi-phi.vercel.app, why we collect it, who it is shared with and what rights you have. We aim to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We do not store your card number or CVV. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart and remember your cookie choice. These are needed for the site to work. Analytics runs only after you press Accept in the cookie banner. You can change your choice any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser and reloading.

3. Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run the site. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – authentication and database (accounts, profiles, orders).
  • Vercel – hosting and, with your consent, web analytics.
  • Resend – sending order receipt emails.
  • Google – Google sign-in if you choose it, and Google Fonts (your IP address is sent to Google to load fonts).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim – converts GPS coordinates to an address when you use auto-detect.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it. Some providers store data outside India.

4. How long we keep it

Account and profile data is kept while your account is active. Order records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

5. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database access is restricted so that each signed-in user can read only their own profile and orders. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and make a complaint. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

farmin is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you on the site.
